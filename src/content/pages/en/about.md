For over a decade, I have been committed to working in mobile app development, with a focus on the {apple} iOS platform.

Today I’m Executive Tech Manager at [Inter](https://inter.co/), where I lead the Core Mobile Platform team, a group of 18 people. I guide our software engineering standards across features, the design system, architecture and performance.

Before that I was the manager of the mobile CORE iOS team for the Inter Super App, a team of eight iOS engineers: standards, reviews, architecture and the release train. I’m still an iOS specialist.

I’ve written software since 2008, web first, then mobile, and iOS since 2011, in Objective-C and then Swift.
